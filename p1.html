Background & approach

C2K resells donated computers primarily through eBay. Today, knowing what is in the warehouse, what has been tested, where a unit sits, and what has already sold depends on memory and manual checking. With hundreds of units on hand and identical models spread across shelves, this costs staff time daily and creates real risk: selling a unit twice, shipping the wrong unit, or losing track of test results that eBay buyers rely on.

We deliberately designed this phase demo-first. Instead of writing requirements in the abstract, we built a working prototype, put it in front of the people who will use it, and revised it from their feedback. This proposal writes down what that process converged on, so the build phase holds no surprises.

Phase 1 is intentionally a tracking and data system, not an automation system. eBay listing and de-listing stay manual in this phase, wrapped in safeguards that make the manual process safe. Automation is Phase 2, and the data model in Phase 1 is designed from the start to support it.

What Phase 1 delivers

Grouped by workflow. Everything below is demonstrated in the current prototype.

1 · Intake & item recordsstaff

  • Every physical unit gets its own permanent C2K ID and printable barcode label, even when several units are the same model. Identical models are grouped in views so lists stay readable at 300 to 400 items and beyond.
  • Item records hold category, brand and model, serial number, condition notes, photos placeholder, and a full status history with timestamps on every step.
  • Snap-the-label assisted entry: photograph the spec sticker and the form pre-fills name, serial, and category for staff to confirm. beta at launch

2 · Shelves & physical locationsstaff

  • Custom-named shelves managed in the app: add, rename, and delete. Each shelf gets a printable label carrying both a scannable barcode and the readable name.
  • Put-away and moves are scan-first: scan the shelf label, then scan each unit going onto it. Manual entry is always available as a fallback when a barcode is damaged.
  • Deleting an occupied shelf never orphans data: its units move to an Unshelved list, surfaced on the home screen until they are re-shelved.

3 · Testing & gradingstaff + volunteers

  • Category-driven test templates: choosing Laptop, MacBook, Desktop, iMac, All-in-One, Mac Mini, or Monitor determines which specs are required, including CPU, memory, storage, battery health percentage, and cycle count for MacBooks.
  • Pass/fail checklist per unit (speakers, ports, camera, keyboard, trackpad, USB, charging). Failed checks flow automatically into the condition disclosure notes used for the listing, so nothing a buyer should know goes unrecorded.
  • Tester initials are required on every completed test, creating accountability per unit.
  • Volunteer mode: volunteers see only the testing workflow, with no access to the rest of the system. Staff review and can edit every volunteer submission.

4 · eBay sales workflow, made safestaff

  • Staff list on eBay as they do today, then bind the eBay listing number to the C2K unit, either one-to-one or one listing covering several identical units as a quantity pool.
  • When something sells on eBay, staff paste the listing number into Fulfill and the system shows exactly which unit or pool it is, grouped by shelf. Take-down is scan-confirmed: scan the shelf, scan the unit, and the right machine goes to packing every time.
  • De-list reminders: when a pooled listing runs out or an in-person sale happens, the system queues a reminder to take the eBay listing down, closing the double-sale gap that manual operations have today.
  • Upload the tracking number and the unit moves to Shipped, completing the record.

5 · Visibility & day-to-day useeveryone

  • Home screen with live stat cards: counts by status and category, unshelved items, and pending tasks, each one tap away from the underlying list.
  • Search across IDs, serials, models, and eBay numbers. Full activity log of who did what, when.
  • Mobile-first design that works one-handed in the warehouse, with a desktop layout for office work. No app store installs; it runs in the browser on any device.

6 · Built for the roadmapfoundation

  • Every step is timestamped and every listing is stored channel-agnostically, so Phase 2 automation (one-click multichannel listing), Phase 3 (the C2K webstore), and Phase 4 (dashboards such as turn rate and aging) plug into this data rather than starting over.
  • Practical consequence: the analytics C2K's leadership will want in Phase 4 are only possible if capture starts now. Data not recorded in Phase 1 cannot be reconstructed later.

Out of scope for Phase 1 (by design, and sequenced on the roadmap): eBay API integration and automated listing or de-listing (Phase 2), AI-drafted listing copy and pricing (Phase 2), the C2K-owned webstore (Phase 3), and the full executive analytics suite (Phase 4; a starter dashboard ships with the care plan below and grows toward it). Barcode scanners, label printers, and other hardware are purchased by C2K directly; we will recommend inexpensive, proven models.

Acceptance, assumptions & dependencies

Phase 1 is accepted when: a unit can be intaken, labeled, tested with a category template, shelved by scan, bound to an eBay listing, found by its eBay number after a sale, take-down confirmed by scan, and marked shipped with tracking, all on a phone, and volunteers can complete tests without seeing the rest of the system.

We assume: Angel remains the point of contact and is available for roughly one to two hours per week during the build; C2K provides sample inventory data and access to its label printer for testing; eBay listing itself remains a manual staff task in this phase, as agreed.

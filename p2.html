Executive summary

Phase 2 turns the tracking system into an automation system. Where Phase 1 makes the manual eBay workflow safe, Phase 2 largely removes it: a tested unit's record, specs, photos, and condition disclosures become an AI-drafted listing that staff review and publish to eBay and the C2K Store in one click, and a sale anywhere automatically takes the listing down everywhere. The de-list reminder queue from Phase 1, the double-sale risk it guards against, and the copy-paste of eBay numbers all disappear.

This is the phase that changes staff economics. Listing one machine on eBay today costs 15 to 25 minutes of a staff member's attention; with drafts pre-written from data the warehouse has already captured, the same listing takes about two minutes of review. Across hundreds of units a year, Phase 2 pays for itself in staff hours alone.

What Phase 2 delivers

1 · One-click publishing

  • From any tested unit's record, one button drafts the complete listing: title, description, and condition notes generated by AI from the specs, test checklist, and disclosures Phase 1 already captured. Staff review and edit before anything goes live; nothing publishes without a human approving it.
  • Publish to eBay and the C2K Store together or separately. Quantity listings for identical models are built automatically from the model group.
  • Bulk mode: select a batch of tested units and publish them as a queue, with progress and per-item results.

2 · eBay connected for real

  • Official eBay API integration: listings are created, updated, and ended from inside the system, with the eBay item number bound to the C2K unit automatically instead of by hand.
  • Sales flow back automatically: an eBay order appears in the Fulfill queue on its own, already matched to the right unit and shelf. No more pasting listing numbers.
  • Price and quantity edits made in the system push to eBay; no double bookkeeping.

3 · Cross-channel sync, the double-sale killer

  • When a unit sells on any channel, including in person at the warehouse, its listings on every other channel end automatically within minutes.
  • Quantity pools stay in sync across channels: one pool of five identical laptops shows the correct remaining count everywhere.
  • Every automated action is logged in the unit's timeline, so the audit trail from Phase 1 stays complete.

4 · Built on Phase 1, feeding Phase 4

  • No migration and no retraining on a new tool: Phase 2 adds buttons to screens staff already use, and the Phase 1 workflow remains available as a manual fallback.
  • Listing, publish, and sale events all land in the same timestamped record, which is what lets the care plan dashboard start showing channel comparisons and time-to-sale.

Assumptions & dependencies

We assume: Phase 1 is live and in daily use; C2K's eBay seller account is in good standing and C2K authorizes API access; eBay's developer approval process cooperates (its lead time is built into the 12-week window); AI drafts are always reviewed by staff before publishing, which keeps C2K in control of what buyers see.

Not included: additional marketplaces beyond eBay and the C2K Store (each can be scoped later as a small add-on), and paid eBay fees or promoted listings, which remain C2K's own seller costs.

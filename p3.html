Executive summary

Phase 3 gives C2K what eBay can never give it: a store of its own. Every sale on eBay hands roughly 13 to 15 percent of the price to eBay in final value fees, on top of presenting C2K as just another seller in a crowded marketplace. The C2K Store is a branded storefront on C2K's own domain where the full price of every sale funds the mission, the story ("Your Purchase Powers Digital Equity") is front and center, and the customer relationship belongs to C2K.

Because the store's catalog comes straight from the Phase 1 inventory system, it can be built in parallel with Phase 1 and launch alongside it. A working preview is already live for you to click through today.

Everything the package includes

1 · The storefront

  • A custom store on C2K's own domain, designed around C2K's brand and mission, with the digital-equity story woven into the shopping experience rather than bolted on.
  • Mobile-first product pages showing the specs, photos, battery health, and honest condition disclosures captured during Phase 1 testing. The transparency that makes C2K trustworthy on eBay becomes the store's signature.
  • Category browsing, search, and filtering across laptops, desktops, Apple, monitors, and accessories.

2 · Checkout & payments

  • Secure Stripe checkout: cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay, with automatic receipts. Standard card processing fees (roughly 3 percent) are the only per-sale cost, versus roughly 13 to 15 percent on eBay.
  • Shipping and local pickup options at checkout, configured for how C2K actually fulfills.
  • Sales tax handled through Stripe's tax tooling.

3 · Run from the system staff already use

  • Publishing to the store is a toggle on the Phase 1 item record: no separate store admin to learn, no product data entered twice, ever.
  • Store orders drop into the same Fulfill queue as eBay sales, with the same scan-confirmed take-down, so warehouse practice does not change at all.
  • When a unit sells anywhere, it leaves the store; full automatic cross-channel sync arrives with Phase 2.

4 · Launch & growth foundations

  • Domain setup, SSL, search-engine basics, and social sharing previews, so listings look right when shared on Facebook or in a newsletter.
  • Traffic and sales analytics wired in from day one, feeding the same record that powers the care plan dashboard.
  • Staff training and a written guide at launch.

The math that matters: at eBay's roughly 13 to 15 percent final value fees, about every $40,000 to $46,000 of sales moved from eBay to the C2K Store pays for this entire build. Everything after that is margin recovered for the mission, year after year. A working preview of the store is already live at store.hashingai.dev/c2k.

Timeline · in parallel with Phase 1

The store is built alongside Phase 1 inside the same 12-week committed window, by splitting the work between the two of us, and launches together with the Phase 1 go-live. This is exactly the sequencing from the roadmap: Phase 1 and Phase 3 in parallel first, so Phase 2's automation can then connect all channels at once. The only external lead times are C2K's domain choice and Stripe account approval, both of which start in week 1.
